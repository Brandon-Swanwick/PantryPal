# PantryPal 🍎🥦

PantryPal is a mobile application designed to simplify home cooking and reduce food waste. By tracking the ingredients you already have, PantryPal suggests delicious recipes you can make right now, helping you save money and use every item in your kitchen.

## 🌟 Key Features

- **Pantry Management:** Easily add, track, and manage your kitchen inventory.
- **Smart Recipe Suggestions:** Discover recipes based specifically on the ingredients you have on hand.
- **Expiry Notifications:** Get alerts when items are near their expiration date to ensure nothing goes to waste.
- **Dietary Preferences:** Filter recipes by dietary restrictions and preferences.
- **Customizable Categories:** Organize your food items in a way that makes sense for your kitchen.

## 🛠 Built With

- **Swift & SwiftUI:** For a smooth, native iOS user experience.
- **MVVM Architecture:** Ensuring clean, maintainable, and scalable code.
- **External API Integration:** Leveraging recipe databases to provide diverse meal options.
